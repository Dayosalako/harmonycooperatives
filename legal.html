Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 07, 2026

At Harmony Welfare Cooperative Society Limited, we are committed to protecting your personal information and maintaining the highest standards of data privacy in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal and financial information including but not limited to: full name, phone number, email address, Bank Verification Number (BVN), National Identification Number (NIN), passport photographs, proof of address, employment details, and bank account information.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

Your data is used solely for processing membership applications, savings and loan transactions, identity verification, risk assessment, regulatory compliance, and to communicate important cooperative updates.

3. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures including encryption, secure servers, access controls, and regular security audits to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information may only be shared with regulatory authorities when legally required or with trusted service providers under strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, update, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@harmonycooperative.com.

Terms of Service

Last Updated: April 07, 2026

By accessing or using the services of Harmony Welfare Cooperative, you agree to comply with these Terms of Service.

1. Membership Eligibility

Membership is open to Nigerian citizens and legal residents above 18 years of age who agree to abide by the cooperative’s rules and make regular savings contributions.

2. Savings and Loans

Loans are issued at a fixed 10% monthly interest rate. Members must maintain consistent savings to qualify for loans. Late repayments attract penalty fees as stipulated in the loan agreement.

3. Member Obligations

Members agree to provide accurate information, repay loans as scheduled, and maintain confidentiality of cooperative information.

4. Termination and Suspension

We reserve the right to suspend or terminate membership for breach of terms, fraudulent activity, or failure to meet financial obligations.

5. Limitation of Liability

Harmony Welfare Cooperative shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from the use of our services.

Anti-Fraud Policy

Last Updated: April 07, 2026

Harmony Welfare Cooperative maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy against all forms of fraud and financial misconduct.

1. Commitment to Integrity

We are dedicated to ensuring transparency, accountability, and trust in all our operations.

2. Prohibited Conduct

  • Submission of forged or falsified documents
  • Identity theft or impersonation
  • Collusive activities to obtain unauthorized loans
  • Money laundering or financing of illegal activities
  • Any form of misrepresentation or deceit

3. Detection and Prevention

We utilize advanced verification processes, background checks, and continuous monitoring to detect and prevent fraudulent activities.

4. Reporting Suspected Fraud

If you suspect any fraudulent activity, please report immediately via:
Email: info@harmonycooperative.com
Phone: +234 803 406 7079 or +234 802 335 8340

5. Consequences of Fraud

Any person found engaging in fraudulent activities will face immediate membership termination, full loan recall, blacklisting, and possible criminal prosecution under Nigerian law.

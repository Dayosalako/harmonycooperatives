Anti-Fraud Policy

Last updated: April 07, 2026

1. Zero Tolerance for Fraud

Harmony Welfare Cooperative has zero tolerance for any form of fraud, misrepresentation, or financial misconduct.

2. Prohibited Activities

  • Submitting false or forged documents
  • Multiple account registrations with false identities
  • Collusion to obtain unauthorized loans
  • Money laundering or terrorist financing

3. Reporting Fraud

If you suspect any fraudulent activity, please report immediately to: info@harmonycooperative.com or call +234 803 406 7079.

4. Consequences

Any member or applicant found engaging in fraud will have their membership terminated, loans recalled immediately, and may face legal prosecution.
